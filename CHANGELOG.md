# Changelog

All notable changes to SafeWord are documented here.

## [1.0.0] — 2026-11-21
### Added
- Family setup wizard: family name, codeword generator (3 memorable words + digit) or custom codeword, backup verification question, family circle with call-back numbers.
- The Golden Rule protocol: no codeword → hang up → call back on the saved number.
- Practice drills: 3 fully scripted scam simulations (fake accident call, "digital arrest" officer, lost-phone-abroad message) with scored choices and explanations.
- In-call panic flow: word-for-word script to say, hang-up guidance, one-tap call-back to family members, outcome logging.
- Scam-message checker: paste any WhatsApp/SMS/email — 13 weighted scam-pattern detectors (digital arrest, OTP theft, fake family emergency, secrecy pressure, urgency, UPI/gift-card/crypto payment, job/task scams, lottery, threats, links) with a 0–100 risk score and verdict. Runs 100% on-device.
- Printable family safety card (codeword optionally included, off by default).
- Full UI in English, हिन्दी (Hindi) and മലയാളം (Malayalam), including drills and checker explanations.
- Help page: what to do if money was sent — call 1930, bank freeze, cybercrime.gov.in (India) plus US/UK reporting.
- Dark mode, offline PWA, Android APK + iOS build workflows, GitHub Pages site. No account, no server, no tracking — all data stays in the browser on the device.
